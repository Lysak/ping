# Privacy Policy — PING

**Effective date:** 2026-09-02

PING is a network utility that sends ICMP echo ("ping") requests to a host you
choose and shows the round-trip time.

## What PING collects

**Nothing.** PING has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash
reporting, and no third-party SDKs. It does not contain any tracking code.

## What stays on your device

- The list of custom hosts you add and which host is currently selected.
- Your theme preference.

This data is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is
never transmitted anywhere and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Network activity

When you press **Ping**, the app sends ICMP echo packets (or, as a fallback, a
TCP connection attempt) to the host you selected, in order to measure latency.
No personal data is included in these packets. The app makes no other network
requests.

## Permissions

- `INTERNET` — required to send ping packets.
